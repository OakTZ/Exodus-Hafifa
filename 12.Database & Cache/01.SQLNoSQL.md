### Goals
- The trainee will understand the difference between SQL and NoSQL

### Tasks
- What are SQL databases? What are NoSQL databases? What are the diffrences between them? Create a comparison table for SQL and NoSQL terms. What are the corresponding terms for Table, Row, Column.
- What are the pros and cons of SQL databases like PostgreSQL compared to NoSQL databases like Redis or MongoDB?
- What types of data or use cases are best suited for NoSQL? What types are best suited for SQL? (ACID vs BASE)
- When would you prefer MongoDB over PostgreSQL? What about Redis? What the use case of each one?
- How do consistency and availability differ in SQL vs NoSQL under network partitioning (CAP theorem)?
