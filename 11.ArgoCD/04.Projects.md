### Goals
- The trainee will understand what an ArgoCD Project (AppProject) is and why we need it
- The trainee will know how to restrict what an Application can deploy, and where

### Tasks
- Read about Projects
- What is the `default` project? What can an Application in the `default` project deploy? Why is that dangerous on a shared cluster?
- Explain the following AppProject fields:
  - `sourceRepos`
  - `destinations` (server / name and namespace)
  - `clusterResourceWhitelist` and `clusterResourceBlacklist`
  - `namespaceResourceWhitelist` and `namespaceResourceBlacklist`
  - `roles`
  - `syncWindows`
- An Application asks for a destination namespace that its project doesn't allow. What happens? Where do you see the error?
- Why would you block cluster-scoped resources (e.g. `ClusterRole`, `CustomResourceDefinition`) in a team's project?
- What is the difference between ArgoCD **project roles** and the global RBAC in `argocd-rbac-cm`?
- What are **sync windows**? Give a real use case
- How would you split projects in a cluster shared by 3 teams?
