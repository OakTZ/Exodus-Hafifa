### Goals
- The trainee will understand the App of Apps pattern
- The trainee will know how to bootstrap a whole cluster from a single Application

### Tasks
- Read about the App of Apps pattern
- Explain the pattern: what does the "root" Application deploy? What do the "child" Applications deploy?
- Draw the tree of Applications and resources for a root app that manages 3 child apps
- What is **cluster bootstrapping**? Which resources do you still have to create by hand, and which can be managed by ArgoCD from then on?
- Can ArgoCD manage itself (its own Helm chart and configuration)? What are the risks?
- In the child Applications, why should `metadata.namespace` be the namespace ArgoCD runs in (usually `argocd`)?
- Why do you usually want the cascade-delete finalizer on the child apps? What would happen if you deleted the root app?
- What are the limits of App of Apps? When does it become painful? (Think about 50 microservices across 5 clusters)
