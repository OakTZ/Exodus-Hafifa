### Goals
- The trainee will understand what GitOps is and which problems it solves
- The trainee will understand the difference between push-based and pull-based deployments

### Tasks
- Read about GitOps (see the references)
- What are the 4 principles of GitOps (OpenGitOps)? Explain each one in your own words
- What is the "source of truth" in GitOps? What does that mean for someone running `kubectl apply` or `kubectl edit` by hand?
- Explain the difference between a **push-based** deployment (e.g. a CI pipeline that runs `helm upgrade`) and a **pull-based** deployment (an agent in the cluster that pulls from Git)
  - Which credentials does each model need, and where do they live?
  - Which model is easier to audit? Which one is easier to roll back?
- What is **drift**? Give 2 examples of how drift can happen in a real cluster
- How do you roll back a bad deployment in GitOps?
- What are the downsides of GitOps? (Think about secrets, emergency hotfixes, and many environments)
- Name 2 GitOps tools other than ArgoCD
